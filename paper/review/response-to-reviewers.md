**R-12 (must fix, blocking B2). Luck-floor degeneracy.** addressed-experiment. Root cause: `RandomAgent` normalizes fully-invested weights, which on a one-symbol universe is deterministically w = r/r = 1.0, i.e. buy-and-hold, for every seed. Fix in `crates/sharpebench-sim/src/agent.rs`: on one-symbol universes the agent now draws its gross exposure per period (flat when u < 0.5, else a uniform long weight), with a regression test (`random_agent_varies_on_single_symbol_universe`) pinning non-degeneracy, per-seed variation, and the flat/long mixture. Multi-symbol behavior is untouched, and the entire sweep was rerun to prove it: all eight multi-symbol dataset files reproduce byte-identically; only `rates-1d.jsonl` changed. Editorial note on "partially commodities": the committed commodities records show five distinct luck-floor raw returns (two symbols give genuine variation), and the rerun confirms bit-identity, so no commodities repair was needed. Results under the corrected floor: rates luck agents are now five distinct streams; best rates luck DSR is 0.000 at every N (the old 0.993 at N=1 vanished with the degeneracy); the falsification invariant (luck floor never beats a reference agent on raw return) now holds on rates as a genuine comparison rather than a tie between clones. Paper changes: Sec. 5 preamble (floor definition), Sec. 5.6 reports the degeneracy, the fix, and the new numbers honestly; Limitations "What changed" lists it as the revision's correction; Appendix C notes the rates dataset requires this randomization to have a control at all. The measured-dispersion path's distinct-streams guard is stated as an arena requirement in Sec. 3.3 (not claimed as built); the source-level fix removes the duplication that motivated it on shipped data.
