\begin{abstract}
Benchmarks for trading agents often rank realized return over short windows, where sampling noise, selection and hidden risk can dominate skill: one widely cited board reports its leading model at a Sharpe ratio of $1.51 \pm 1.08$ and its buy-and-hold baseline at $0.02 \pm 0.87$, intervals that overlap. SharpeBench is a deterministic, judge-free scoring kernel whose rank predicate has five conjuncts: deflation for search, $\text{pass}^{k}$ across execution seeds and out-of-sample windows, a process audit, a stationary block-bootstrap null, and the configured mandate. Raw return never ranks. We validate the protocol on author-written reference agents, a risk-managed control and seeded zero-skill agents over nine frozen datasets spanning four asset classes and four bar sizes; no paid frontier-model result is reported. The first finding is a dimensional pitfall: applying an annualized deflation prior to a per-period Sharpe made the old daily and hourly bars correspond to annualized Sharpes of $18$ and $106$. The repaired records serialize each cell's configured, measured or measured-and-floored dispersion source and annualized bar. Under the default configuration, no agent is eligible in any of 4{,}608 scored agent--configuration cells: every named reference is below deflation and fails the all-run regime-robustness verdict. The never-catastrophic and benchmark-relative verdicts also admit none. The risk-managed control is process-clean and respects its per-run drawdown cap on weekly US indices, but it fails deflation, bootstrap and the applicable reliability verdict; it is not a deflation-only refusal. A separately calibrated synthetic witness establishes a nonempty acceptance region at injected per-period Sharpes of $0.35$ on weekly and $0.20$ on daily geometries. Nine self-audit attacks are demoted, while identity governance remains the stated residual Sybil limitation. Across 1{,}000 zero-skill agents on two daily datasets, no agent is eligible; on daily crypto an intentionally unfloored diagnostic peaks at DSR $0.2500$, while the operational dispersion floor lowers the maximum to $0.0012$, far below the $0.95$ gate. Two empty pre-entry forward-window records were explicitly superseded when scoring provenance changed, so no window is currently open and no forward result is claimed. Every reported number is generated by a listed command from committed data and a content-hashed source snapshot.
\end{abstract}
