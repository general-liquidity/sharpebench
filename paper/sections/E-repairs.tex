\section{Repairs made after the evidence was frozen}\label{app:repairs}

Every table and figure of \cref{sec:experiments} except the synthetic witness was produced by the frozen v0.9.0 snapshot. This appendix records the repairs that landed afterwards, what each changes in what the current engine would compute, and what was and was not regenerated. None of it changes a number reported in the body: where a repair would move a published number, the number is reported as it was computed and its provenance is stated here.

\paragraph{The first repair round.} An independent audit of the 0.18.4 tree in September 2026 found defects in code that produced numbers reported here, and the repairs merged after the evidence was frozen. Three change what the current engine would compute. The standardized-moment estimators mixed finite-sample normalizations, so every probabilistic and deflated Sharpe value in this paper, together with its standard error and any interval the kernel would attach to it, was computed under the superseded convention. Refreshing the two deterministic score fixtures under the corrected estimator moves those statistics and leaves the eligibility and ordering of those two fixtures unchanged. The frozen evidence itself was not rescored, so how far the corrected estimator would move the deflated Sharpes in the tables is not established, and no statement in this paper that a verdict is unchanged by the repair extends beyond those two fixtures. The simulator let a zero target cross through flat into a short position, which changes simulated trajectories and removes residual trading that the momentum entrant's no-op events had counted. That entrant also ignored its declared lookback, and rescoring the synthetic fixture under the repair reorders two ineligible display rows and changes field-relative diagnostics while leaving all four eligibility and ordinal values as they were. A fourth divergence is recorded rather than resolved: on the commodities panel the current simulator reconstructs two finite-Sharpe streams where the frozen default carries eight measured-dispersion rows, so that panel now has insufficient measured support and falls back to the configured prior.

\paragraph{The synthetic witness, the one regenerated artifact.} A fifth defect affected the synthetic witness of \cref{sec:witness}, and it is the one case where the committed evidence was regenerated rather than left frozen. The producer derived every return stream from an expression that overlapped the calibration-member index with the execution-seed index, so the five zero-edge calibrators that fix the dispersion bar shared draws: the frozen predecessor of the committed field was drawn under those collided seeds and carried thirteen distinct streams per window where the design calls for forty. The corrected producer derives each stream from an unambiguous domain, member, window and execution-seed tuple, and the field reported in \cref{sec:witness} is a declared rerun under it, performed on the same machine and recorded as a new experiment rather than as a correction of what the predecessor measured; every one of its forty streams per window is distinct in each of the three synthetic populations. The crossing point moved by exactly one grid step on both geometries: from a per-period Sharpe of $0.35$ to $0.40$ on the weekly geometry (annualized $2.52$ to $2.88$) and from $0.20$ to $0.25$ on the daily geometry (annualized $3.17$ to $3.97$). The calibrated dispersion bar did not move, because in both draws the measured zero-edge dispersion fell below the precommitted annualized floor of $0.5$ and the floor set the bar; what moved was the per-run $\passk$ leg, which needs one more step of injected edge before all 48 runs clear the marginal PSR. The rerun executed the current kernel, which also carries the corrected standardized-moment estimators, so the one-step shift is the joint effect of the independent draw and the corrected estimators and is not attributed to the seeds alone. Both are single draws. The interpretation is unchanged: the acceptance region is nonempty, and in the committed draw its boundary lies one grid step higher than the predecessor placed it. No other table in this paper has been regenerated. Rerunning any other field would be a new empirical run rather than a correction to what the frozen evidence measured, so those numbers are reported here with their provenance stated instead of being silently refreshed. The repository's audit checklist tracks the disposition of every finding, including those still open.

\paragraph{A constant track is refused rather than scored.} The \texttt{hold} reference agent never trades, so its returns are identically zero and its Sharpe ratio is undefined. The frozen v0.9.0 kernel returned a Sharpe ratio of zero for such a track, so all 576 frozen \texttt{hold} records carry a PSR of $0.5000$ against zero and 528 carry a nonzero deflated Sharpe, values of an undefined statistic. A kernel repair after the freeze refuses a constant observed track for PSR and DSR with the typed reason that a constant series has no Sharpe ratio. The pooled score then reports a DSR and PSR of $0$ with that deflation error, no DSR interval and a deflation bar of $0$; it is not rank-eligible, its disqualification reason is the deflation's unavailability, and a constant run fails its per-run reliability test. The bootstrap is unchanged, because the mean of a constant series is defined. The repair tests exact equality of every observation with the first rather than a zero computed variance, because a constant nonzero series has a tiny nonzero computed standard deviation: before the repair, a track of $0.001$ repeated 408 times scored a Sharpe ratio of about $1.5 \times 10^{15}$ and a PSR and DSR of $1.0$, and six such runs were rank-eligible under the default configuration. No frozen agent had a constant nonzero track, so that path moves no reported number, and no frozen eligibility verdict changes: every \texttt{hold} record already fails $\passk$ with a bootstrap $p$ of $1$. The current kernel would refuse all 576 \texttt{hold} records as unavailable; the frozen count of records with a deflated Sharpe of exactly zero is left as computed. The benchmark-relative verdict refuses a zero-excess series through its own explicit dispersion clause, independently of this repair (\cref{sec:relative}).

\paragraph{A second repair round, and what it does and does not move.} The paragraphs above are the ledger of one audit and not of every repair since. A further independent review in September 2026 produced a second round, merged after the evidence above was frozen, and none of it regenerates a number reported here. Most of that round hardened refusal paths that the evidence never exercised: withholding the whole field-wide snooping family when its shared bootstrap boundary fails, deriving the effective seed width from validated run keys and refusing a contradictory flag, refusing unsupported seed-bootstrap intervals at both language boundaries, preserving a baseline score and pass rate as unavailable rather than as a numeric zero, refusing unobserved or overflowing candidate utilities, retaining each imported column's own observed date axis, naming statistical disqualification reasons instead of returning an empty list, and validating computed quantities in the checked deflation path before a floor or a saturated cumulative normal can conceal an overflow. The evidence above was produced by the example sweep rather than by these boundaries, so none of the tables moves; what changes is what the current engine would do with an input the frozen evidence never contained. Five later findings round out the same review, and two of those are worth naming because they change what the engine reports rather than what it computes. The disqualification taxonomy published \texttt{SelectionUnavailable} as a hard reason while the scorer never gated on the selection axis at all, so a submission whose candidate set refused could be printed as disqualified beside a rank-eligible verdict; the reason is now advisory, which is the label consistent with the scorer, and no eligibility, ordering or score in this paper depends on it because that taxonomy is a legibility layer that computes nothing new. The refusal path of percentile selection, which ranks candidates on a percentile of their bootstrapped utility after \citet[p.~11]{oliveira2025bootstrap}, reported an alpha warning unconditionally, so a refusal caused by too few observations blamed an alpha that was never the problem; the flag now reports where alpha sits and nothing else. The remaining three are internal: a checkpoint schema version raised so that a checkpoint predating the persisted per-round budget is refused rather than granted a fresh round, a corrected label on four finite-computation checks that validate an agent mean rather than a field maximum, and the deletion of two contract legs in one resume path that compared a contract's fields against themselves. None of them touches the scoring kernel, the goldens, the examples or the frozen evidence.

\paragraph{Later rounds.} Subsequent rounds through the 0.26.0 release repaired the host-observed gateway's money accounting, closed fail-open paths in reporting types and refused malformed configurations, and added the receipts, controls and diagnostics described in \cref{app:mechanism}. For a valid configuration none of them moves a gate of \cref{eq:eligible}, a ranked board's eligibility or order, or a committed evidence artifact. The same rounds corrected the unit conversion of the separate single-track honesty verdict, which changes that verdict's outputs; that surface produces no number in this paper.

\paragraph{A track with no Sharpe ratio no longer votes on other agents' deflation bar.} The refusal recorded above stopped the kernel scoring a constant track's own deflated and probabilistic Sharpe. It did not stop that track voting on everyone else's. The field-measured dispersion drew its sample from every entrant whose pooled Sharpe evaluated finite, and a track with no Sharpe ratio evaluates finite in both of its forms: an identically zero track takes the zero-variance sentinel and votes a Sharpe of zero, and a constant nonzero track votes the value its rounded mean leaves, on the order of $10^{15}$. Because that sample sets the deflation bar for every agent on the panel, one entrant could move every other entrant's verdict. Measured on the current tree before the repair, adding one entrant whose track was the single number $0.001$ repeated beside five dispersed agents raised the measured dispersion to $6.3\times10^{14}$ and drove every other agent's deflated Sharpe to zero; adding an identically zero entrant moved the same field's dispersion from $0.1800$ to $0.2240$ and its leader's deflated Sharpe from $0.9998$ to $0.9629$. Ranking now qualifies the dispersion sample through the same predicate the kernel refuses a track's own deflation on, before clone collapse and before the five-vote minimum, so that each cluster's median is taken over streams that vote and the vote count checked against the minimum is the size of the sample its standard error is claimed for. An excluded entrant is still scored, still ranked and still refused on its own terms. Where the exclusion leaves fewer than five votes the panel falls back to the configured prior and stamps \emph{configured} rather than measuring a rump field. Two adjacent statistics read the same degenerate stream the same way and are repaired with it: field crowdedness counted a constant nonzero stream as a correlated peer of every agent at a Pearson correlation near $10^{-17}$, the residual its rounded mean leaves rather than a measurement, and the revealed-selection elicitor valued a declared candidate with a constant nonzero stream at its $10^{15}$ sentinel, which dominated every real option and reported the submitted track as an economic-dominance violation. Neither can move a record on a field in which every stream has a Sharpe ratio.

\paragraph{What that repair does to the frozen evidence, and what cannot be recovered from it.} The \texttt{hold} reference agent is in the ranked field of every panel in this paper, not only the rows it leads, and its pooled track is identically zero, so it voted on the dispersion wherever a record stamps \emph{measured} or \emph{floored}. That is 64 of the 576 default-sweep panels, being all sixteen host configurations on each of commodities (floored) and hourly crypto, daily FX and daily rates (measured), together with all 27 panels of the externally specified sweep and four of the nine power-curve panels. On the three crypto panels of the externally specified sweep a second entrant did the same: \texttt{faber-10m}'s record carries a raw mean return of exactly zero and a worst-window drawdown of exactly zero, which together admit only an identically zero track. The risk-managed gate panels are the exception among the measured records: their seven-agent field contains no \texttt{hold} and no other constant track, so their stamps stand unchanged. How far the repaired measurement would move each affected bar is not established here and is not estimated. The committed records serialize the dispersion actually used, the bar it implies and each agent's deflated and probabilistic Sharpe, but not any agent's pooled Sharpe, and the probabilistic Sharpe cannot be inverted for one without that track's skewness and kurtosis; the seed-leg records carry per-seed run statistics of a different three-dataset field rather than the seed-averaged pooled tracks the vote is taken on. Even the direction is undetermined: withdrawing an observation at zero from a sample standard deviation can raise or lower it, depending on the remaining values. Two consequences are established, both about which path the current engine takes rather than about any value it would produce. The reconciliation \cref{sec:mandate} gives for \cref{tab:eligibility} against \cref{tab:mandate} is a property of the frozen kernel. It rests on the ninth agent supplying a fifth surviving dispersion vote on daily US indices, 4-hour crypto and weekly crypto; withdrawing \texttt{hold}'s vote takes every one of those three panels below the minimum in the nine-agent field as well as the eight-agent one, so on the current engine the added agent changes the dispersion source on no panel, and the three panels that measure in the eight-agent field (hourly crypto, daily FX and daily rates) are the three that measure in both. The regression test that reconstructs both fields now asserts that. Separately, the commodities panel, which already reconstructed too few streams to measure under the repaired simulator, now reconstructs one. Nothing in this paper was rescored. The two committed golden fields hold four and three agents and take the configured path, so neither moves; \texttt{sharpebench score -{}-json} on the example submissions is byte-identical; the committed WebAssembly bundle is rebuilt because the kernel changed.

\paragraph{Repairs merged after the 0.27.0 release.} Five later repairs change what the engine refuses or reports, and none of them moves a number in this paper.
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=1pt]
\item A period repeated within one run is refused by both import parsers and by the keyed scoring path. That path had accepted a repeated period when only one agent declared periods or when every agent repeated the same one, so one period's return entered the mean, the variance, the PSR sample length and the bootstrap twice. The frozen evidence came from the simulator, not from an import.
\item Forecast comparisons use each pair's own support (\cref{sec:forecast-quality}). The two committed tutorial reports move to schema v2 with the same support, because both fields are complete, and the archived v1 pilot report still verifies.
\item Windows that overlap or are listed out of time order are refused as scoring evidence at the boundaries that know window coordinates (\cref{sec:stats}). Every frozen market window is adjacent to the next.
\item A track with no Sharpe ratio is never a Pareto member. Before the repair, a track that never trades could not be dominated, because nothing beats zero drawdown and zero turnover, so it was always marked optimal. The synthetic golden field changes only in the Pareto flag of \texttt{hold}, no gate reads that flag, and the committed WebAssembly bundle is rebuilt. A later repair corrected the rule in both directions. Membership now reads the kernel's single statement of whether a track has a Sharpe ratio, which refuses fewer than two observations as well as constant and non-finite tracks, where the earlier test read a probabilistic Sharpe that does not refuse a one-observation track, so such a track stayed on the front beside its own deflation error. And a refused track that has an observation dominates again: its return, drawdown and turnover are all defined, so an agent it beats on all three is not optimal, while a track with no observation has no return to compare and dominates nobody. That second correction leaves the committed golden where it was, because \texttt{hold} dominates only \texttt{random}, which buy-and-hold dominates as well.
\item Calibration counts only the confidences an agent stated, and captured trajectories move to contract schema 3 (\cref{sec:stats}). The synthetic golden field changes in its calibration counts, Brier scores and confidence-weighted return, none of which a gate reads, and the committed WebAssembly bundle is rebuilt again. The frozen evidence carries no calibration field.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{A third round, on the same subsystems.} Four further repairs landed after the ones above, and none moves a number in this paper either. A stated confidence is now graded on the book its own decision left, read before anything trades at the next bar and including the dividends that book collects there. The return booked at the next step, which the previous rule used, also carries the next decision's fills, fees, financing and borrow, and misses a dividend when the next decision exits before the engine credits it; a decision whose order the execution noise delayed, or whose partial fill left a remainder, now opens no pair at all, because the book that gets marked is not the book that decision chose. The synthetic golden's outcomes move accordingly and \texttt{momentum}'s Brier score moves with them, from $0.247334919619079$ to $0.24343824540169307$; \texttt{buy-and-hold} and \texttt{random} state a constant $0.5$, whose Brier is $0.25$ whatever the outcomes. A run that states no confidence at all now weighs the submission's mean stated weight in the confidence-weighted return rather than dropping out of it, so a failing sentinel run that states nothing can no longer be averaged away. A period that two different windows of a keyed field declare is refused by both the import parsers and the keyed scoring path, which extends the within-run refusal of the first item above to the across-window case. And the forecast report gains a declared contract plan: under schema v2 the field support is the union of resolved digests, so one document could resolve an invented contract and charge every other agent a gap against a support it had itself defined. A \texttt{sharpebench.forecast-contract-plan.v1} file now names the digests every agent must answer, gaps are measured against the plan, resolved digests outside it enter no loss or comparison, and a planned report is schema v3 because its support means something a v2 reader would misread. Without a plan the report is v2 byte for byte, so the two committed tutorial reports and the archived v1 pilot are unchanged.

\paragraph{A fourth round.} The last repairs merged before this revision, none of which moves a number in this paper either. The constant-track refusal now reads each window as well as the concatenation, so a track constant at a different level in every window, which pools into a series that varies, no longer earns a Sharpe ratio, a deflated Sharpe or a dispersion vote (\cref{sec:predicate}). On a seven-agent test field with a measured annualized bar of $1.3692$, one such entrant had taken the bar to $36.7192$ and every honest deflated Sharpe to exactly zero while scoring $1.0000$ itself. A calibration outcome is now also net of the financing and short borrow the held book paid, which the previous ratio cancelled, and a book at a net asset value that is not positive opens no pair; the reference agents neither lever nor short, so the golden does not move. A keyed field's windows are pooled in the order each agent declares them rather than in the lexicographic order of their labels, which had let a relabelling from \texttt{w1} to \texttt{w01} move a maximum drawdown from $0.30$ to $0.51$ without changing one return, and a field whose agents declare different orders is refused. The timing reference and the forecast report now state how far their own figures can be trusted (\cref{app:capabilities,sec:forecast-quality}), and the arena's intake closes two routes around a commitment and stops certifying a board with no rows (\cref{sec:selfaudit}).
